# El estilo de Kinhold para Meshy: la maqueta en miniatura

valravnx, 10 nov 2026: el prompt de estilo que va delante de cada encargo a Meshy (diseñado con otra IA y elegido por él).
No se cambia sin él. Cada encargo es «este prompt + la cosa concreta».

## El prompt entero (para generar la imagen de referencia de cada cosa)

A handcrafted, realistic miniature medieval world, resembling an exceptionally detailed historical architectural diorama brought to life.

Believable medieval construction, historically grounded materials, proportions and craftsmanship. Refined, slightly simplified geometry, never generic low-poly. Handcrafted imperfections, irregular edges and subtle asymmetry.

Natural muted earthy palette: weathered oak, aged timber, straw, limestone, clay, linen, wool, leather and moss. Rich but restrained material detail, visible wood grain, sculpted wear, natural surface irregularities and tactile textures.

Matte and natural material response. Soft, physically believable lighting. Strong readable silhouettes and carefully controlled detail density. Every object should feel like a carefully crafted miniature replica of something that genuinely existed in a medieval settlement.

Consistent miniature scale, visual language, material quality and artistic direction across all assets. Museum-quality medieval miniature craftsmanship. Grounded historical realism with a slightly stylized miniature aesthetic.

NOT cartoonish, NOT chibi, NOT fantasy, NOT steampunk, NOT plastic toy-like, NOT glossy, NOT generic low-poly, NOT exaggerated proportions, NOT photorealistic full-scale photography.
