1. Diagnosis: what we had and what was missing

GDD v0.6 has the rules of each system. The analysis document had lists of content. What almost all of them lacked was the concrete workings: which tool gets each thing, how much it yields, at which station it is made, what it costs, how long it lasts. This document covers that as far as needed to start and move forward.

2. Technology tiers APPROVED (provisional)

The GDD says progress runs from the Early to the Late Middle Ages through stations, and that the boundary stone is upgraded with materials of the technology tier. For both to work, concrete tiers are needed. I propose five:

Each tier is entered by building a specific station, and that station needs materials from the previous tier. This way progress feels natural and there is no tech tree to memorise.

5. Agriculture: sowing and harvesting PROPOSAL

DECIDEDOne season per real month; yield by climate band (good, poor, unviable).

The cycle

  1. Seeds. Three sources: wild plants of your biome (wild cereal and pulses, wild vegetables), NPC merchants (seeds from other bands) and keeping part of your harvest.
  2. Prepare the plot. The unit is a 2 × 2 m crop tile, on the same grid as building. With a hoe it is prepared tile by tile; with a plough and a draught animal, several at once (for large fields).
  3. Sow in season. Each crop has a sowing window. Outside it, the seed does not germinate.
  4. Tend. The biome's rain waters on its own; in the south, in summer, you must water with a bucket or an irrigation channel. Manure from your animals increases yield.
  5. Grow. The crop advances through visible stages (sprout, growing, ripe). If winter arrives in the north before harvest, it is lost.
  6. Harvest with the right tool (table).
  7. Process the harvest to make it useful (threshing, milling, drying, retting…).
  8. Store seed for the next sowing and the produce in a granary or storehouse.

11. A player's first day

A check that everything fits together: what someone does in their first session with what is defined above.

  1. Appears in a linen shirt and trousers, barefoot, at an accessible spot in their country.
  2. Thirst warnings: finds a river and drinks with their hands.
  3. Pulls up fibre and makes cord; ties the cord on as a belt (1 slot).
  4. Gathers flint and stone, sets up a knapping stone and makes a flake and a stone axe.
  5. Gathers branches, makes a firewood frame, lights a campfire for the night.
  6. Plants their boundary stone (3 stones) and builds a branch shelter, which binds as a bed.
  7. With the fire-hardened spear hunts a hare, butchers it with the flake and roasts it.
  8. Fells a young tree, makes the log bench and enters N1.

No step needs anything that is not defined. From there on: the log cabin, the basket, the spade and the first crop tiles.
