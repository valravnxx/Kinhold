1. Summary of the analysis

GDD v0.2 has the pillars and the big systems well defined (world, building, combat, animals, social, offline defence). What it lacks falls into three kinds:

  • Whole systems not mentioned that a game like this needs in order to work: character progression, economy and trade, sea travel, decay of what is built, map and orientation, locks, communication, monetisation, and whether solo mode is a separate world or the same online world. They are in section 2.
  • All the concrete content: what is gathered, what is crafted, with what, at which workstation. Sections 3 to 15 hold a proposed catalogue, thought out with medieval realism and real production chains. It is there to prune: strike it out, extend it or change it.
  • A foundational decision that changes the whole catalogue: which medieval period is depicted. The longsword belongs to the Late Middle Ages; technology, armour, weapons and architecture change a lot from century to century (question 1).

About the catalogue: it is written with realism as the guide. Being listed does not mean it must go in; on the contrary, the less there is at the start, the sooner it is playable. What is marked under "Basic" would be the minimum for the first milestones.

11. Siege equipment PROPOSAL

  • Battering ram (hand-held and with a mobile roof): against gates and palisades.
  • Scaling ladders: getting over walls without breaking them.
  • Mantlet: mobile shield for getting close.
  • Siege tower: advanced.
  • Mangonel / trebuchet: against stone; very advanced and expensive.
  • Sapping: mining under a wall (does it go in?).
  • Fire: against wood, if it is decided that fire does damage.

14. Food, drink and preservation PROPOSAL

  • Cooking: roast meat and fish, porridge, pottage, bread (flour + oven), flatbreads, soup, cheese, butter, sausage.
  • Drink: water, milk, beer (cereal, hops in the north), cider, wine (south), mead.
  • Preservation: salting (needs salt), smoking, drying, pickling, storing somewhere cool. Fresh food spoils over real time.

15. Healing wounds PROPOSAL

It fits combat without bars: each wound is treated with its own remedy.

  • Bandaging (cloth): for cuts and bleeding.
  • Stitching (needle and thread): deep cuts.
  • Cauterising (iron in the fire): stops severe bleeding, leaves a lasting effect.
  • Splinting (splints and bandages): fractures.
  • Poultices and infusions (herbs): infection, fever, faster recovery.
  • Rest: wounds heal over time; eating and sleeping well speeds it up.

16. All the questions, numbered

Answer them by number whenever you like, in whatever order you like. Those in blocks A and B are the ones blocking the first milestone.

A. Project foundations

  1. Period. Which centuries does it depict? For example: Early Middle Ages (Vikings, mail, wooden castles), High (mail, stone castles) or Late (longsword, plate, cranequin crossbow). It can be a progression within the game.
  2. Solo mode. A world of its own with no server in the browser, or the same online world where you play isolated? Does progress carry over from one to the other?
  3. Visual style. Low-poly 3D made in code, third-party/commissioned models, or isometric 2D.
  4. Platforms. PC only, or also phone and tablet?
  5. Fantasy or pure realism? Is there anything supernatural (creatures, magic) or is it 100% invented history?

B. Building (milestone 1)

  1. Grid or free placement.
  2. Removing pieces. Can you dismantle a piece that holds up others? Is material recovered when dismantling?

C. Character and progression

  1. Does the character improve? Skills that rise with use, trades, or nothing (only the player improves)?
  2. If there are skills: a total cap that forces specialisation (and depending on others)?
  3. Do strength and endurance rise with training (affects how much you carry)?
  4. Durability and repair of tools, weapons and armour?
  5. How are technologies unlocked: by use, by discovery, by players teaching each other, by stations built, or a mix? Do they belong to the character or to the family?
  6. Does item quality depend on material, tool and skill?

D. Survival and death

  1. Which needs are simulated: hunger, thirst, cold/heat, fatigue, sleep, illness?
  2. How are they shown without bars: only on the character or with some discreet indicator?
  3. On death: where do you respawn, what do you lose, what stays on the ground? Permanent death of any kind?
  4. Logged-out character: does it disappear, or sleep in the world where it can be attacked?
  5. Does food spoil over time?

E. World and travel

  1. Day/night cycle: at what pace compared with real time?
  2. Seasons and weather: do they exist? How long does a game year last?
  3. Map: complete from the start, drawn by exploring, or none?
  4. Do players build roads and bridges?
  5. Can you sail the sea and rivers? With which vessels?
  6. Are there NPCs apart from sentries (villages, traders, bandits)?
  7. Is there wildlife that attacks (wolves, bears, boars)?
  8. Can trees be planted, and do they grow just as slowly? Do ores and stone run out?

F. Economy

  1. Is there currency? Do players mint it (with silver) or is it barter?
  2. Markets or stalls that work without the owner online?
  3. Does each country have exclusive resources to force trade?

G. Social, lands and PvP

  1. How is land claimed? Is there a size limit?
  2. Vassalage: what does it mean in mechanics (tributes, defence, permissions)?
  3. Do alliances turn off PvP between allies?
  4. Does what is built decay if nobody maintains it? What happens to abandoned bases?
  5. Locks with keys on doors and chests, permissions by family, or both?
  6. Siege: can a base be besieged while its owners are offline, or do only the sentries hold it back? Which milestones unlock sentries?
  7. Does fire damage wooden structures?
  8. Communication: global chat, proximity chat, voice, messengers/letters?
  9. Family heraldry (coats of arms, banners, colours)?

H. Product

  1. Character creation: what is chosen (appearance, sex, age, country)?
  2. Interface: how do inventory and crafting look without a classic HUD?
  3. New players: tutorial, protected starting zone, or nothing?
  4. Monetisation: free with something paid (cosmetic, subscription), one-off purchase, subscription?
  5. Rules: how are cheating, multiple accounts and toxic players handled?
  6. Languages of the game.
  7. Minimum hardware that must run it.
  8. Sound and music: what style?
