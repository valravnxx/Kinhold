## About (descripción)

Kinhold is a medieval survival game you play in your browser, alone or with a friend, on PC, phone or tablet. No fantasy, no health bars: from the High to the Late Middle Ages, you gather what the land gives, build your home with your own hands and fight with real fencing technique.

It is an independent project, designed by valravnx and developed with AI assistance, completely in the open: the roadmap, the work diary and every playable version are public at playkinhold.com, in English, Spanish and Russian.

Your support pays for what keeps it going:
- the online server where we all play together,
- the art of the game (3D models of nature, buildings, animals and characters),
- and the time to keep building it.

Every coffee helps. Thank you for being part of it.
